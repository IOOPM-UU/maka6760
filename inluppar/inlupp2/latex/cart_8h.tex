\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Shopping carts of the webstore. 

NOTE\+: The draft of the documentation for this module was created with Claude (Anthropic). It has been proofread and changed by us afterwards.

\begin{DoxyAuthor}{Author}
Markus Karlsson and Alva Fällman Nilsson 
\end{DoxyAuthor}
A cart is a hash table that maps merch name (string) =\texorpdfstring{$>$}{>} amount (int). Carts are stored in the store\textquotesingle{}s cart\+\_\+ht and are identified by a cart number, which is handed out by ioopm\+\_\+cart\+\_\+add\+\_\+new. Numbers start at 1 and are never reused.

Putting merch in a cart reserves it\+: the amount in all carts together can never exceed the stock. Stock is not decreased until the cart is checked out.

The cart keeps its own copies of the merch names, so the names passed to these functions do not need to outlive the call.
