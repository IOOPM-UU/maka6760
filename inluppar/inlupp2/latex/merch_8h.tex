\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Merchandise and its storage in the webstore. 

NOTE\+: The draft of the documentation for this module was created with Claude (Anthropic). It has been proofread and changed by us afterwards.

\begin{DoxyAuthor}{Author}
Markus Karlsson and Alva Fällman Nilsson 
\end{DoxyAuthor}
A merch has a name (unique in the store), a description, a price and a list of the shelves it is stored on. A shelf has a name and a quantity. A shelf name is one capital letter followed by two digits, e.\+g. "{}\+A01"{} (see ioopm\+\_\+merch\+\_\+is\+\_\+shelf), and a shelf holds at most one kind of merch.

The merch in a store is kept in store-\/\texorpdfstring{$>$}{>}merch\+\_\+ht, and which merch occupies which shelf is also kept in store-\/\texorpdfstring{$>$}{>}storage\+\_\+ht. The functions here keep the two consistent, so use them instead of changing the tables directly.

Ownership\+: a merch owns its name and description, so these must be heap-\/allocated (e.\+g. with strdup) and are freed by ioopm\+\_\+merch\+\_\+destroy. Once a merch has been added to a store with ioopm\+\_\+merch\+\_\+add, it belongs to the store.
